Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Information we collect

When you create an account, place an order, make a reservation, or book catering/hotel services, we collect your name, phone number, email address, and delivery/event details necessary to fulfil your request.

How we use your information

We use your information to process orders and payments, manage reservations and bookings, send order/reservation updates, respond to enquiries, and — where you've opted in — send promotional messages.

Payment information

Payments are processed by Paystack. We never store your card details on our servers.

Third-party services

We use Supabase (database & storage), Paystack (payments), Resend (email), and Google Gemini (our AI assistant) to operate the platform. Each provider only receives the information necessary to perform its function.

Your rights

You may access, update, or request deletion of your account information at any time from your account settings, or by contacting us.
