Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026

Order issues

If there's a problem with your order — missing items, incorrect items, or a quality issue — please contact us within 24 hours via our contact page or by phone, and we'll make it right.

Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled before they are confirmed by our kitchen. Once an order is being prepared, it can no longer be cancelled.

Payment problems

If a payment was deducted but your order doesn't show as paid, contact us with your payment reference and we'll investigate and refund you if the payment cannot be matched to a valid order.

Reservations & bookings

Table reservations do not require payment and can be cancelled any time before your reservation. For catering, event, and hotel bookings, refund terms will be confirmed directly with our team based on the specifics of your booking.
